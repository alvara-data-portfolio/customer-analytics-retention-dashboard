# Customer Analytics & Retention Dashboard

## Project Overview
This Power BI portfolio project explores customer behaviour, segmentation, retention, and acquisition opportunities using a fictional dataset. It demonstrates how customer data can support business decisions through interactive dashboards and analytical insights.

## Business Objectives
- Analyse customer behaviour and segmentation.
- Explore customer retention patterns.
- Understand customer acquisition channels.
- Identify growth opportunities and areas for improvement.
- Present business insights through interactive Power BI reports.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualisation
- **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and business metrics
- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset preparation

## Dashboard Highlights
- Customer analytics and retention overview
- Interactive dashboard filtering using slicers
- Customer retention and segmentation insights
- Acquisition channels and growth opportunities
